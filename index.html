Glossary — read this first

Every term used on the dashboard, explained in plain language. Tap a term to jump to its full entry in the user manual.

Observed Exposure

The headline score, 0–100%, for each occupation. It is the share of an occupation's job tasks that (a) an LLM is theoretically capable of doing AND (b) we already see being done by Claude in real Anthropic usage data. Tasks that are automated count double tasks that are augmented. Defined by Massenkoff & McCrory (Anthropic, March 2026).

Theoretical β

A task's potential AI exposure on a 0 / 0.5 / 1 scale, from Eloundou et al. 2023. A score of 1 means an LLM alone can at least double the task's speed; 0.5 means it can with extra tools; 0 means it can't. Used as the upper bound.

Capability gap

Theoretical β minus Observed Exposure. Big gap = the AI could do this work but isn't yet. The leading indicator: when this gap closes, displacement risk rises.

Automation vs Augmentation

Automation: the AI does the task end-to-end. Augmentation: the human and AI collaborate. Anthropic's March 2026 release shows augmentation accounts for just over half of Claude.ai conversations.

Top-quartile exposure

Workers whose occupation sits in the highest 25% of Observed Exposure scores in their region. The group most likely to feel AI impact first.

Hire rate (22–25)

The percentage of workers aged 22 to 25 who started a new job in an exposed occupation in a given month. The earliest moving signal in the US data.

Attribution confidence (1–5)

How confident we are that a layoff was actually caused by AI rather than labelled "AI-driven" for PR. 5 = automation directly cited by company filings or layoff letters. 1 = vague reference in press, plausible "AI washing".

AI washing

When companies attribute job cuts to AI to mask routine cost-cutting or post-pandemic rightsizing. Layoffs.fyi reports ~48% of 2026 tech layoffs are "AI-attributed" but a stricter count puts genuine automation closer to 20%.

O*NET / SOC / NCO / ISCO / ANZSCO

Occupation classification systems. O*NET is US; SOC2020 is UK; NCO-2015 is India; ISCO-08 is the EU; ANZSCO is Australia/NZ. Each region's data is crosswalked to O*NET for comparability.

BLS / ONS / NSO / Eurostat / ABS

Government statistics agencies for the US, UK, India, EU, and Australia respectively. They publish the official labour-force surveys that anchor each region.

AUI

Anthropic AI Usage Index. A country's Claude usage relative to its share of the global working-age population. Singapore leads at 4.19x, US at 3.69x, India ranks 101st of 116 per-capita.

Cadence

The reporting cycle. Weekly: regional digest refreshed every Monday. Monthly: per-region deep-dive. Yearly: retrospective and forecast.

AI Pressure Index & pillar signals

The Simple view's headline layer. Four pillar signals — Job displacement, Hiring pullback, Early-career squeeze, AI adoption pace — each score 0–100 against fixed calibration bands, and combine (30/25/25/20%) into one AI Pressure Index per region. Computed live in your browser from the raw KPIs; switch to Deep view and expand "Under the hood" on any card to see every input, band, weight and source. Each score shows what share of its weight comes from measured sources.

Sector pulse & Sector AI Exposure

AI impact by sector (banking, insurance, IT, manufacturing…) across all six regions. The exposure index weights each sector's occupation mix by the Anthropic Observed Exposure scores — shown within-region only (top sector = 100). Sector pressure blends that (45%) with posting trend or hiring momentum (25%), vacancy/employment momentum (15%) and announced layoffs as a share of the sector's workforce (15%, where published: US via Challenger, EU via Eurofound). Components a region doesn't publish have their weight redistributed — the Deep-view lineage lists exactly what was used and what was missing. Click any sector chip for its occupation make-up and live signals; Deep view adds the full lineage and the region heatmap. Section-level statistics combine some sectors (e.g. UK banking + insurance share section K) — marked "section-level score".

Measured vs Modelled

Each KPI tile carries a badge. Measured: the value is pulled weekly from the named source's published data (Indeed Hiring Lab, BLS, ONS, Eurostat, ABS, Challenger). Modelled: a synthetic placeholder series — no public weekly source exists yet for that KPI in that region. The "early-career unemployment delta" uses a published proxy: youth minus overall unemployment rate, since no statistics agency publishes unemployment by AI exposure.

How the score is calculated — expand

Observed Exposure (occupation) = Σ over tasks of [ time_share(task) × coverage(task) ]

where coverage(task) = 1 if the task has β ≥ 0.5 and sufficient Claude usage in the latest Anthropic Economic Index, weighted by:

  • 1.0 if the use is fully automated
  • 0.5 if the use is augmentative
  • 0 otherwise

Occupation-level scores are aggregated to category level by current-employment weight. See the user manual for the full appendix and citation chain.

Two-track model

Structural signals move slowly and reliably; market signals move fast and noisily. The tool keeps them separate so fast noise never overrides slow signal.

TrackRefreshSourcesUse case
Structural exposureMonthlyAnthropic Economic Index, Eloundou β, O*NET, official occupational projectionsMaintain the watch list, deep-dive reports
Market signalWeeklyIndeed Hiring Lab, Naukri JobSpeak, Seek, Layoffs.fyi, news feeds, ONS Vacancy Survey, ABS Job VacanciesTrigger alerts when an exposed occupation breaches its band
